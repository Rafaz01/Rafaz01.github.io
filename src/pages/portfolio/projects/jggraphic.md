**JG Graphic Creative Studio** is a branding and web design agency based in Gainesville, FL. The studio helps businesses with brand identity, web design and development, social media, SEO, and graphic design.

I designed and developed the agency's own website. The goal was a bold, modern site that shows the studio's personality, presents its work, and turns visitors into project inquiries.

## 🧩 What I Did

- **Next.js build** — developed with the Next.js App Router and React, using built-in image optimization for fast WebP delivery
- **Tailwind CSS design system** — bold typography, a bright orange/blue/dark palette, and reusable components across every page
- **Bilingual support** — English and Spanish versions (`/es`) with a language toggle and proper `hreflang` tags
- **Homepage sections** — Hero with service cards and stats, scrolling service ticker, Services, Featured Work, About, Process, Testimonials, and closing CTA
- **Portfolio** — portfolio page with filterable categories and individual case study pages for each project
- **Blog** — blog section for content marketing and SEO
- **Project inquiry flow** — "Start Your Project" page with a lead form (with honeypot spam protection) and a WhatsApp shortcut
- **Social proof** — client testimonials linked to the studio's Google reviews
- **SEO optimization** — metadata, Open Graph tags, and multilingual indexing
- **Responsive design** — fully optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop

## 💡 Technologies Used

- Next.js
- React
- Tailwind CSS
- next/image (image optimization)
- SEO optimization

## 🧠 What I Learned

Building the agency's own site meant being both the client and the developer. I had to turn the studio's identity into a design system that stands out but stays consistent. I also learned to handle **internationalized routing in Next.js** (EN/ES) and to set up a **case-study portfolio and blog** that can keep growing as the agency takes on new projects.
