**NASH Natural Care** is a haircare brand rooted in Dominican heritage. It offers natural haircare essentials like a botanical scalp oil, a daily growth tonic, and complete haircare collections.

The client needed a Shopify store to launch their first product line as an **early-access "private release"**, so they could sell to their community right away while the full store was being prepared.

## 🧩 What I Did

- **Shopify store setup** — configured products, collections, checkout, and payments (Shop Pay, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and major cards)
- **Dawn theme customization** — customized the Dawn theme to match NASH's clean, minimal brand aesthetic
- **Custom early-access section** — built a custom Liquid section for the pre-launch experience, with an editorial hero that tells the founder's story
- **Product bundles** — set up the Complete NASH Collection and Pre-Wash Growth Pack with sale pricing
- **Email capture** — early-access signup form to build the launch list for the official store opening
- **Bilingual experience** — Spanish storefront interface with English product content for the brand's audience
- **Responsive design** — optimized for mobile shoppers coming from social media

## 💡 Technologies Used

- Shopify
- Liquid (Shopify templating language)
- CSS
- JavaScript

## 🧠 What I Learned

This project taught me how to plan a **staged e-commerce launch**. The store had to sell real products right away while still feeling exclusive and pre-launch. Building a **custom Liquid section** on top of Dawn showed me how to extend a Shopify theme without breaking its updates and core features like the cart and checkout.
