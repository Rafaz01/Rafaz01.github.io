**WoodTryst** is a custom cabinetry and cabinet refinishing business in the Memphis, TN area. They offer cabinet refinishing and refacing, custom cabinetry, built-ins, bathroom vanities, and custom storage, all finished with a professionally sprayed conversion varnish.

The client needed a premium website that shows the quality of their craftsmanship and turns local homeowners into consultation requests.

## 🧩 What I Did

- **Custom build** — hand-coded, lightweight HTML/CSS/JS site with no CMS overhead
- **Premium visual design** — warm wood-tone palette, elegant serif typography (EB Garamond + Poppins), and decorative wood-grain line art in the hero
- **Project galleries** — Kitchen, Built-ins, and Furniture galleries with a custom JavaScript lightbox
- **Before & after showcase** — paired galleries for real kitchen projects, with details like project type and duration
- **Services section** — seven service cards, each leading to the consultation form
- **Lead-qualifying form** — "Request a Free Consultation" form with city, service interest, and timeline fields, processed server-side with PHP
- **Social proof** — five-star Google reviews section linked to the business's Google profile
- **Local SEO** — service-area section covering Germantown, Collierville, East Memphis, Cordova, Lakeland, and Arlington
- **Click-to-call** — phone CTAs in the header and hero for mobile visitors
- **Responsive design** — fully optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop

## 💡 Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript (ES6+)
- PHP (form handling)
- SEO optimization

## 🧠 What I Learned

This project showed me how to design for a **high-ticket local service**. The visuals have to feel as crafted as the product, and the form has to qualify leads (city, service, timeline) so the owner can prioritize calls. I also built a **lightbox and gallery system from scratch** to show a large portfolio of project photos without slowing down the page.
