**Reinigungsexperten** is a mobile cleaning service based in Zurich, Switzerland, specializing in car interiors, sofas and upholstery, mattresses, and carpets. The team comes directly to the customer's home or office with professional spray-extraction equipment and eco-friendly products.

The client needed a fast, trustworthy website for the Swiss market that clearly explains the services, shows real results, and makes it as easy as possible to request a quote.

## 🧩 What I Did

- **Custom build** — hand-coded, lightweight HTML/CSS/JS site with no CMS overhead
- **Bilingual support** — German (primary) and English versions with an EN/DE language switcher and `hreflang` tags
- **Conversion-focused layout** — Hero, Services, Before & After, Process, About Us, Service Area, FAQ, and Contact sections
- **Before & after slider** — interactive comparison slider to showcase real cleaning results
- **Quote request form** — contact form (name, phone, email, postal code, service, message) processed server-side with PHP
- **WhatsApp integration** — floating WhatsApp button so customers can send photos of the item to be cleaned
- **Local SEO** — service-area section covering Zurich and surrounding municipalities, plus Open Graph metadata for social sharing
- **Performance** — optimized WebP images and minimal JavaScript for fast load times
- **Responsive design** — fully optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop
- **Legal pages** — Privacy Policy and Impressum as required for Swiss businesses

## 💡 Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript (ES6+)
- PHP (form handling)
- SEO optimization

## 🧠 What I Learned

This project taught me how to build for a **multilingual European market** structuring content and `hreflang` tags properly so each language version is indexed correctly. I also built a **before/after comparison slider** from scratch and learned to design around local trust signals (fixed pricing, service area, Impressum) that matter to Swiss customers.
